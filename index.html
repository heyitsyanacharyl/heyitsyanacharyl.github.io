How I Work.

The Clear Content Method

01

Discovery

We start with a quick call or written brief where you share your site, goals, and current SEO pain points. I review your existing content and identify where the biggest gaps are: outdated pages, weak rankings, or missing optimization.

02

Setup

In the first few days, I organize a task tracker in Trello or Airtable, confirm priority pages and keyword targets, and align on how often we'll check in. Everything is documented so nothing gets lost or repeated.

03

Execution

This is the ongoing work: writing, optimizing, and publishing content directly in WordPress on a consistent weekly schedule. I follow the agreed specs and checklist closely, so what you get matches what we planned.

04

Reporting

At the end of each week, I send a short update covering what was completed, what's scheduled next, and anything that needs your input, sent through email or whichever channel you prefer, like Slack or WhatsApp.

How I turned a common customer question into SEO-ready content for Big Easy Kitchens.

Problem

Big Easy Kitchens needed content that could capture homeowners actively deciding between a full kitchen remodel and a partial renovation, a high-intent, comparison-style search topic people look up before they're ready to book a consultation. The goal was content that could rank for this decision-stage query while guiding readers naturally toward the brand's services.

Solution

  • Researched and targeted high-intent keywords around kitchen renovation comparison searches
  • Drafted the article with AI assistance, then proofread and edited it for accuracy and natural, non-AI-sounding copy
  • Strengthened internal linking across Big Easy Kitchens' related service pages and sub-brand content
  • Optimized on-page elements: headers, meta title, and content structure, for readability and search visibility
  • Published directly in WordPress, formatted for skimmability with clear sections

Result

The article went from assignment to live publication in under 24 hours, giving Big Easy Kitchens a new decision-stage entry point connected to their service pages through stronger internal linking, with no gap in their content coverage for this high-demand search topic.

How I helped Prosple maintain accuracy across 500–600+ monthly job listings — without sacrificing speed.

Problem

Prosple needed to post and maintain a high volume of internship and graduate job listings for US-based roles every month, a workload where accuracy and consistency easily slip when speed is also required. Manually tracking hundreds of listings without a structured system risks duplicate posts, outdated listings, and formatting errors that hurt applicant engagement.

Solution

I managed the full listing workflow in the CMS: posting new roles, optimizing job descriptions using structured formatting guidelines, and tracking assigned tasks and progress in Airtable to avoid duplication or missed updates. I followed consistent formatting standards across every listing to keep the front-end display clean and readable at scale, checking employer names, job titles, and listing details for accuracy before publishing.

Result

Filtered CMS records show sustained monthly volume in the 500 to 600+ range, with one tracked period reaching 688 listings, all managed and maintained without losing consistency across postings.

Job Posting & Employer Profile Creation for Prosple.

Problem

At Prosple, a job listing could only go live once the employer's profile was published. That meant getting the employer profile setup right came first, before any job could be posted under it.

Solution

  • Created and published new employer profiles in the CMS, structuring company details, descriptions, and formatting to match platform standards
  • Reviewed and published job listings under those profiles, keeping formatting, structure, and detail consistent with each employer's information
  • Checked employer details and listing content for accuracy before anything went live

Result

Since job listings depend on the employer profile going live first, getting the profile setup right the first time kept the whole posting process moving without delays.
